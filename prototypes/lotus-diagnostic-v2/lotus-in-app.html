Aarav · Class 8B

Why the mist and not the frogThe frog moved one pad per answer, which only counts questions, and the count changes as Lotus adapts. The mist shows how much Lotus has learned about each skill, so progress is honest even when the length changes.

Why it never feels like a scoreA confident wrong answer clears the mist as fast as a right one, and "I don't know yet" clears it too. A guess clears a little. Buds open because Lotus understands them, not because the student got them right.

Where the motion goesMotion only marks evidence: a ripple to the skill the answer was about, mist drifting away, a bud opening. The games stay calm. No timers, no lives, no confetti on right answers.
