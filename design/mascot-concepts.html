Cogna · mascot exploration

Four friends who could teach Grade 3 maths

Each concept ties to something Cogna already says about learning: the light coming on, growth through mistakes, mastery opening up, and one step at a time. Lumi and Axel have hand-drawn concept art with an expression sheet. Lo and Step are ideas only so far.

Lumi

a firefly

Her tail glows brighter as an idea clicks. The "light comes on" moment, made visible.

Why it fits
Pairs directly with the forty-lights story on the landing page: every child is a light, and Lumi is one of them.

Rive rig
Glow intensity is one number input (mastery 0–1). Small silhouette, easy to fly across a lesson.

Axel

an axolotl

Axolotls regrow what they lose. Axel shrugs off a wrong answer and tries again.

Why it fits
A growth-mindset character for the moments that matter most: when a child gets it wrong. Kids already love axolotls.
Moods
gills flutter curiousoops wrong answerregrow retryspin correct
Rive rig
Six gill fronds give lots of expressive motion from one bone chain each. Lives in water, so lessons can float.
Idea only. No illustration yet.

Lo

a lotus bud

Opens one petal per skill mastered. By the end of the unit Lo is in full bloom.

Why it fits
Lotus is already the name of the student journey and report. The mascot becomes the progress bar.
Moods
closed new topicpeek tryingpetal skill wonbloom unit done
Rive rig
Petal count driven by a number input; leaf arms do the waving. A seasonal colour swap is cheap.
Idea only. No illustration yet.

Step

a tortoise

Carries the steps on his back, one book at a time. Never rushes, never skips one.

Why it fits
Echoes the landing headline word "step" and the carry-steps work in lessons. Calm and patient for anxious learners.
Moods
tuck in hard problempeek hintstack step donestretch solved
Rive rig
Book stack grows per step completed. Slow walk cycle is cheap to animate and reads as "take your time".

The owl prototype

The owl in apps/web/rive/owl is well drawn, but an owl as the learning mascot of a playful edtech app will be read as Duolingo's at a glance, especially with the Duolingo-like art direction we're aiming for. If we keep it, it needs a twist that only Cogna has. Otherwise one of the four above gives the brand its own face.

Recommendation

Lead with Lumi, keep Lo as the runner-up

  1. Lumi already belongs to the brand story (forty lights) and owns a metaphor no big edtech uses.
  2. Her glow is a single, honest signal of understanding, which maps cleanly onto mastery data.
  3. The simplest silhouette of the four, so it works as an app icon, a loading spinner, and a 24px avatar.
  4. Lo is the strongest fit if Lotus stays the product's central name.
