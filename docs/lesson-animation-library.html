Lesson Animation Library

Six animation templates the lesson-writing AI can choose from. The AI doesn't draw anything itself. It picks the template that matches where a student's working broke, then fills in the student's own numbers. The existing lesson checker verifies every value before anything is rendered.

Proposal: previews only, not built into the app yet
1 · DiagnoseThe diagnostic finds the first line where the student's algebra broke.
2 · PickThe AI matches the mistake to one template below.
3 · FillIt fills the template's slots with the student's own equation and steps.
4 · VerifyCode re-checks every number. If anything fails, the lesson isn't shown.

Template 1

Balance Scale

The equation sits on a scale. A change made to only one side tips it, and making the same change on the other side levels it again.

Picked when
The student did something to one side of the equation and not the other.
P5_EQUALITY_BALANCEADD/SUBTRACT_BOTH_SIDES
AI fills in
  • left, rightthe two sides before the step
  • operatione.g. −3, ÷2
  • skippedSidewhich side the student forgot
Pilot fit: Divya

Template 2

Sign Carrier

The factor outside the bracket becomes a token that visits each term inside, carrying its sign. The term where the sign was lost flips over to the right value.

Picked when
A negative factor was multiplied in, but a sign inside the bracket came out wrong.
LIN_DISTRIBUTE_NEGNEGATIVE_DISTRIBUTIONFND_SIGN_MUL_DIV
AI fills in
  • factorthe number outside, with its sign
  • termseach term inside the bracket
  • wrongTermthe student's value and the correct one
Pilot fit: Aarav

Template 3

Multiplier Sweep

An arc draws from the outside factor to each term it multiplies. A term the student skipped stays grey in a dashed box until its arc arrives, and a counter shows how many terms are done.

Picked when
The student multiplied some terms in a bracket but not all of them.
LIN_DISTRIBUTE_POS
AI fills in
  • factorthe number outside the bracket
  • termseach term inside
  • skippedwhich terms the student left alone
Pilot fit: Meena

Template 4

Plug It Back

The student's correct steps get ticks first. Then their answer goes back into the original equation, the two sides don't match, and the one arithmetic slip gets fixed.

Picked when
The method was right, but the final arithmetic gave the wrong number.
SUBSTITUTE_CHECKDETERMINISTIC_ARITHMETIC
AI fills in
  • originalthe starting equation
  • studentLinestheir working, line by line
  • studentAnswer, answerwrong and correct values
Pilot fit: Rohan

Template 5

Error Ripple

Shows the student's whole working, flags the first line that broke, and tints every later line that followed from it. A corrected path then branches off the last good line.

Picked when
One early mistake carried through otherwise correct steps. This is also the fallback for any single break that the other templates don't cover.
COMBINED_ALGEBRAany first break
AI fills in
  • studentLinestheir working
  • breakLineindex of the first wrong line
  • fixedLinesthe corrected continuation
Works for any student

Template 6

Step Builder

Solves the equation one change per line and labels each change. Partway through, it pauses for the student to choose the next line. Wrong options come from common mistakes, and a wrong pick replays that step.

Picked when
There isn't enough evidence to name a mistake, or the student needs a calm restart. The checkpoint pause can also be added to any other template.
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCEINSUFFICIENT_OR_CONFLICTING
AI fills in
  • equationthe problem to solve
  • stepseach line and the change it makes
  • checkpointwhich step pauses, plus 2 wrong options
Pilot fit: Kabir

What the AI sends back

Instead of writing a whole lesson freehand, the AI returns one small choice per scene: a template name plus its slots. The renderer draws the animation and the narration follows its beats. Before rendering, the lesson checker confirms that every number in the slots matches the student's real working and the correct math.

  • One template per lesson keeps it focused. Two at most, e.g. Error Ripple, then Plug It Back.
  • Every template ends on the corrected line, so no lesson ends on the mistake.
  • New templates can be added later without changing how the AI works.
