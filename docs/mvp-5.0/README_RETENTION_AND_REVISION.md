# MVP 5.0 — Retention & Revision

> Multi-subject retention estimates; modality may support review but does not replace spaced practice evidence.

## Extensions

- Per-concept retention remains primary
- Optional modality review assets for high-priority retention
- Workload caps include modality minutes (convert to question-equivalent)

## Non-goals without new formula version

- Unversioned mastery calendar decay
- Treating video watch time as independent CORRECT evidence

**Modality dwell / mastery research note:**

Video or animation dwell time alone does NOT count as mastery evidence under `mastery-formula-v2` (or any version through MVP 5.0 unless explicitly updated). Only retest attempts after modality exposure contribute to mastery updates. This is an **open research question**: correlating dwell × retest-lift may inform a future mastery formula version, but remains unsolved/deferred unless pilot data enables validation. See [`README_PERSONALIZATION.md`](./README_PERSONALIZATION.md) for rationale.
