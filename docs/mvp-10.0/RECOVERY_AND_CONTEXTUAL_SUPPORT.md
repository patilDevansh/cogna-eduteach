# Recovery, observation history and contextual support

Canonical additive MVP10.0 amendment, authorised30October2026. Extends LEARNER_EVIDENCE and MODULE_LEARNING without replacing diagnostic-cycle or teaching-first contracts. Existing LLM prohibitions on guessing mood, attention and effort remain. No new flat uiAction is introduced.

## Recovery and presentation

Exercise maintains a server-owned current-delivery projection whenever content is resolved. A GET session returns it without selecting a replacement item. Browser recovery is tab-scoped, learner/mode-bound,24hours maximum; it must confirm ownership, active session and matching issued question, or an exact previously committed pending event, before restoration. Exact pending requests take priority over editable drafts. Lost acknowledgement replays the original receipt. Drafts are never graded or labelled server-saved. Lotus restores through its existing authenticated GET, scoped by learner/topic/assignment/fake-or-live backend, including class resumes. Pending question1 stays separate from an optimistic question2 preview. Tile picks must restore visibly against issued bounds. Stale, expired, unavailable or malformed recovery remains explicit; local cache is not question or grading authority.

A first visible, answerable presentation may be recorded independently of submission through learning-evidence/observations. Queued events retain their original identity/context and are bounded locally; unanswered presentation is not proven abandonment. Prefetched/generated/hidden-only content is not seen. Existing actual answers/help and versioned context remain in their original pipelines. History joins those records for the same signed-in student, preserves purpose, versions, missingness and source IDs, paginates sources, and withholds answer keys. Teacher/parent rollout requires their separately verified relationship access.

### Review amendment: draft process and answer visibility (2 November 2026)

For active, issued Exercise and Lotus questions, a settled draft can produce a `DRAFT_CHECKPOINT` observation bound to the signed learner, session, question and version. The client records a candidate only after a short typing pause or field blur; a cleared field can be recorded as `CLEARED`. This is a bounded snapshot, not a keystroke stream or a scored attempt. Candidate text is limited to 200 characters; longer work remains local. Each checkpoint carries its own identity and elapsed wall time from the current question episode. Exact retries are idempotent, changed payloads and other learners are rejected, and old questions cannot accept new checkpoints. On Check, the client freezes the answer and interaction timing, then waits up to two seconds for its checkpoint acknowledgement before sending the answer. An outage or timeout cannot block the maths answer indefinitely; the checkpoint may remain pending and later be rejected if the question advances. Do not claim that every draft is durably saved. A rapid edit followed by closing the tab may also never reach the server. Local draft recovery remains a separate feature, and server checkpoints never replace the latest recoverable draft.

The student history displays their submitted answer text for existing Exercise and Lotus attempts, alongside elapsed time when collected. Draft snapshots are marked unsubmitted and ungraded. Elapsed time includes possible notebook work, hidden time or interruption; it is not an attention-span score. Existing independent exit seals continue to withhold correctness and future answer keys. Student-only access does not grant parent or teacher access to draft values. Capture limits, access and retention for broader school rollout remain a separate governance decision.

## Contextual support policy v1

Deterministic policy contextual-support-v1 operates separately from diagnostic AI. Explicit frustration/confusion/tiredness/interruption/notebook/continue choices are SELF_REPORTED. Two distinct verified incorrect practice questions among the last3on the same concept may trigger neutral help: REPEATED_VERIFIED_DIFFICULTY, never a frustration diagnosis. Thresholds and5minute proactive cooldown are product defaults to evaluate. After an offer, a new proactive offer also requires a new verified wrong answer; unchanged old evidence is not enough after cooldown. Explicit learner requests remain available. Speed, notebook silence, hidden/offline durations and confidence alone do not infer attention or affect. Insufficient or contradictory cognitive evidence stays UNKNOWN.

State chain: observation/check-in → offer → accepted/declined → delivered → optional experience recheck and separately observed fresh mathematics result. All stages bind learner/source/session/question/version/content/policy. Same-ID identical retries are idempotent; conflicting reuse is rejected. Session-scoped database locking serialises proactive offers and transitions. Decline or correction never changes ability scores. A hint must be in the offer, available from reviewed practice content and linked to actual server issuance. Diagnostics permit pause/continue, not instructional hints or answer leakage. Breaks save/retain work and can resume; network failure must not turn local progress into a false saved claim. Delivery retries never issue a second hint.

Self-report BETTER/SAME/WORSE is experience evidence. The next different-question result on the same concept can be linked as fresh practice, with recorded hints and purpose; it is not automatically independent evidence, mastery, retention or proof that support caused learning. No return means an unknown outcome. Existing independent exits and fresh delayed-review eligibility/sealing remain unchanged. Retention/memory capacity and attention-span improvement cannot be inferred from this support loop.

## Research basis and limits

Worked-example/scaffold support is motivated by [Sweller & Cooper1985](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1207/s1532690xci0201_3). Regulation rather than automatically removing challenge is consistent with [D’Mello et al., Confusion can be beneficial for learning](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959475212000357). These studies support intervention families, not Cogna's detector thresholds, effectiveness claims or a child's fixed learning style. [Rohrer et al.2015](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED557355.pdf) studied grade7mathematics interleaving; it motivates future fresh mixed practice but does not validate this policy. Clinical labels and automatic emotion diagnoses are out of scope.

### Explicit practice recheck

After confirmed support, an optional SUPPORT_CHECK requests a reviewed same-concept, same-difficulty typed item not previously recorded as answered/viewed by this learner and not the current issued item. It is PRACTICE, not an independent exit. Server selection, existing current-delivery projection and source-choice linkage are saved atomically; no candidate means unavailable, with original work retained. The resulting verified answer can be joined to this exact check. Ordinary later concept answers are labelled later recorded answers, never automatically fresh checks. This does not establish delayed retention or causal effectiveness.
